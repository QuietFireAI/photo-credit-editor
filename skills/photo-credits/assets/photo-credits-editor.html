Review a site folder or local repository checkout. See where likely photos appear, check what credit is recorded, and export the updates you approve. The scan reads only the folder you choose. Your photos stay on this computer.

Review the excluded assets here. Include any that should be treated as photos.

    What counts as complete: record the credit and license or permission. For Creative Commons, include the source URL and license version, plus the title for versions 3.0 and earlier. Check the evidence before approving a credit.

    A credit line says who made a picture. It is not permission to use it. If you are not sure you are allowed to use a picture, check with its owner before publishing.
