# Support

## Get help

- **Email:** support@quietfireai.com
- **Report a bug or ask for a feature:** open an issue at https://github.com/QuietFireAI/photo-credit-editor/issues

Start with the [User Guide](docs/USER-GUIDE.md). Its troubleshooting section covers the common problems.

## What to include

- What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- Which part: the editor, the site script, the scanner, or Claude following the skill
- Your browser and operating system, for editor or site problems
- The output of the scan, for scanner problems

Please do not send passwords or other private information.
