# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-12

First release.

- Skill `photo-credits`: scan a site, add missing entries, fill credits without inventing them, show credits on the site, check before launch.
- `scan_photos.py`: inventory a website folder against `photo-credits.json`, with `--write`, `--check`, `--json`, `--exclude` and `--min-kb`.
- Photo Credits Editor: single-file browser form with previews, filters, a license field, a "no credit needed" option, and unsaved-work recovery.
- `photo-credit.js`: slideshow captions, picture captions and a credits page list, configured with `data-` settings.
- Sample site in `examples/sample-site`.
