# Code of Conduct

This project is for anyone who wants to give photographers proper credit. To keep it a good place to work:

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep discussion on the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeat the behaviour.

To report a problem, email support@quietfireai.com. Reports are kept private.
